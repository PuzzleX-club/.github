This document describes the lightweight contribution terms for PuzzleX public repositories.

1. you have the legal right to submit the contribution
2. the contribution is your original work, or you otherwise have the right to license it
3. you grant PuzzleX and all recipients of the project the right to use, modify, distribute, and sublicense your contribution under the same license terms that apply to the target repository

## No Transfer of Ownership

You keep ownership of your contribution. This document only grants the rights needed for the contribution to be merged, distributed, and maintained as part of the repository.

## Employer / Organization Contributions

If your contribution was created in the course of employment or on behalf of an organization, you confirm that the relevant employer or organization has authorized you to submit it under these terms.

## Scope

These terms are intended to support routine open-source contributions to PuzzleX public repositories.

They do not replace any separate commercial agreement, partnership agreement, or private contract that may apply in other contexts.
